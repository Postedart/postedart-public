# How to create and send a Postcard in Posted.art

To create a personal Postcard in Posted.art, choose artwork from the library, add your message on Write, preview it on Share, create the Postcard and send its link to your recipient. The main flow is **Home → Card → Write → Share**.

You do not need to upload artwork or buy an Art Certificate to make a personal Postcard. **Create & Earn** is a separate feature for artists submitting original work to the shared library. **Art Certificates** are optional NFT purchases for collectors.

## 1. Choose artwork on Home

Open **Home** using the house icon. Browse the existing images and videos, or use Search to find a category or series. Select the artwork you want to use; it opens on **Card**, headed **Explore**.

Telegram starts on Account and connects automatically. Select Home to begin the same browsing flow. iOS, Android, web/PWA and Farcaster use the matching shared browsing controls.

[Home — choose existing artwork](screenshots/shared-home-controls-visible.png)

For filtering and finding a full series, see [Home, Search & Series](home-and-series.md).

## 2. Continue from Card to Write

On Card, select the **top-right arrow** or the **bottom pencil icon** to open Write. You can use the artwork in a personal Postcard without purchasing it.

The artwork price and any NFT purchase control on Card relate to optional Art Certificates. They are not the step for making a personal Postcard.

[Card — view the selected artwork](screenshots/shared-card-eur-price.png)

For artwork details and Favorites, see [Card: View Artwork](artwork-view.md).

## 3. Add your personal message

On **Write**, enter your message. A new iOS message can start ready to type; on the web canvas, select **Aa** to edit text. Use the **Done editing checkmark** to finish text editing.

You can also add stickers, change the canvas background, attach music or a voice recording, or add a gift image. Those attachments are optional.

[Write — edit your message](screenshots/shared-write-initial-text-editor.png)

[Write — canvas tools](screenshots/shared-write-canvas-tools.png)

See [Write & Personalize](write.md) for formatting, positioning and attachment instructions. Match the screenshots to the current editing state; they do not represent every client's keyboard.

## 4. Review the Postcard on Share

Open **Share** using the top-right arrow or the bottom paper-plane icon. Review the selected artwork, your message and any attachments.

[Share — preview with optional music and gift](screenshots/shared-share-music-gift.png)

The preview screenshot includes optional attachments; your controls depend on what you added.

## 5. Create the Postcard

On Share, select the **dollar sign** or **top-right chevron** to open confirmation. These controls create a personal Postcard; they are different from an Art Certificate purchase on Card.

The next panel depends on your client and account state:

| Client | What happens next |
| --- | --- |
| Standalone iOS or Android app | Choose **Send Postcard now** for this flow. Scheduling is a separate native-app option. Follow any account or subscription prompts shown. |
| Web wallet, including inside Farcaster | Review the eligible free offer or the paid wallet checkout. Connect and sign in if prompted; paid creation requires approval in the wallet. |
| Telegram | Review the eligible free offer or the paid Telegram Stars checkout. A paid checkout hands off to Telegram's payment flow. |

Opening a confirmation panel alone does not complete a payment. Review the offer and follow the applicable confirmation steps. Allow the app to finish preparing the Postcard. See [Preview & Share](share.md) for the matching client-specific checkout screenshots and [Pricing & Hosting](pricing.md) for the current FAQ links.

## 6. Send the finished link

When **Postcard is Ready!** appears, use its **Share** button to choose a messaging app and recipient, then finish sending there. Alternatively, use the Postcard link or show the QR code for someone to scan.

[Finished Postcard — QR code, link and Share](screenshots/shared-postcard-ready-qr.png)

Creating the Postcard does not automatically deliver it to anyone. Opening the device's share menu alone does not send it either.

The Postcard link opens the recipient experience. You can also reopen a created Postcard from **Account → Your Postcards** by selecting its circular thumbnail while it remains hosted.

## Which screenshots apply to my client?

The shared Home, Card, Write and Share images illustrate matching interface states across iOS, Android, web/PWA, Telegram and Farcaster. Account, authentication, scheduling and payment prompts require the matching client-specific instructions. See the [Screenshot Reference](screenshot-guidance.md) when selecting an image.
