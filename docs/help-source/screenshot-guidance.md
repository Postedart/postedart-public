## Select the matching image

1. Identify the client: **iOS, Android, web/PWA, Telegram or Farcaster**. Ask if that distinction changes the instructions and the client is unclear.
2. Match the task and state: connected/disconnected, subscribed/unsubscribed, editing/created, and audio or gift present/absent.
3. Use **shared-** images for matching common Home, Card, Write and Share controls across all five clients. The original capture device does not restrict this reuse.
4. Use client-specific images for Account, authentication, Settings, billing, scheduling and wallet approval. Do not present another client's system prompt as the user's own screen.
5. When no matching image exists, explain the step in text. An unrelated screenshot can be more confusing than no screenshot.

The focused text-editor image shows active editing, rather than every client's initial Write state or keyboard. A saved audio clip differs from the initial trimming view. The finished gift panel is read-only, unlike its upload panel. Shared screenshots do not imply that every attachment or feature is present for every user.

## Current prices and networks

Use [FAQ — current prices](https://github.com/Postedart/postedart-public/blob/main/docs/faq.md#how-much-does-it-cost) and the current checkout/store confirmation for amounts. Screenshot prices and Stars totals are examples, not authoritative current offers.

Use [FAQ — supported networks](https://github.com/Postedart/postedart-public/blob/main/docs/faq.md#which-networks-are-supported) for current support. A network list in an image is not a permanently complete list. Distinguish personal Postcard payments from Art Certificate/Create & Earn support, and check the network actually connected rather than inferring it from the wallet brand or host app.

### Client-specific limits on image reuse

- **iOS and Android:** Account, sign-in, Settings and billing use their own images. No iOS subscription-offer image is available; use text rather than the Android offer. iOS scheduling illustrations are not Android system-picker screenshots.
- **Web EVM:** use wallet Account, contribution, NFT and paid-wallet examples for matching supported EVM states. The MetaMask prompts are desktop MetaMask examples, not universal wallet or Farcaster prompts.
- **Solana:** use matching shared browsing/editor/result images. A connected Solana Account, SOL checkout and approval screenshot are unavailable. Do not substitute EVM NFT collections, ETH/BNB amounts or Telegram identity/Stars screens. See [Solana](solana.md).
- **Telegram:** use its connected header and Stars checkout images. Do not use native Settings/scheduling or EVM Art Certificate screens. See [Telegram](telegram.md).
- **Farcaster:** use shared controls and the wallet-interface examples appropriate to the active network. Its approval UI can differ from MetaMask's extension. See [Farcaster](farcaster.md).

The free-confirmation image below applies to eligible EVM, Solana and Telegram web-app flows. It is not a paid Stars or wallet checkout, or a native send/schedule panel. Free completion can say Payment Confirmed without a wallet charge and has no wallet receipt link. Paid Postcard success includes a transaction receipt; NFT success also includes an NFT-view link.
